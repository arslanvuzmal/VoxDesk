# VoxDesk AI — Portfolio Video Script (55–70 Seconds)

**Owner / Presenter:** Arslan Vuzmal Lone  
**Target Duration:** 60 Seconds

---

## Script Sequence

- **0–7s**: "Businesses lose valuable client opportunities when calls arrive after hours or staff cannot respond immediately."
- **7–15s**: "VoxDesk AI answers every inbound enquiry through a configurable AI voice receptionist tailored to your business."
- **15–27s**: "The agent greets the caller, answers approved business questions, and collects essential contact and project details."
- **27–39s**: "It queries calendar availability in real time, presents valid non-conflicting slots, and confirms appointment bookings."
- **39–49s**: "For sales enquiries, VoxDesk calculates BANT lead qualification scores and categorizes high-value leads automatically."
- **49–59s**: "When a caller requests a person, the system prepares a complete context-rich handoff briefing for your team."
- **59–68s**: "Every call is recorded as a searchable speaker-separated transcript, Zod structured summary, and CRM activity log."
